This chapter summarizes and interprets the results presented in
Chapter~\ref{ch:results}, integrating evidence from the ASOCA and MACS-18
cohorts, the synthetic validations, and the pilot SUS study. It evaluates the
pipeline's operational reliability, geometric accuracy, and suitability as a
clinical decision-support tool based on the experimental findings. The
discussion ends by documenting the main limitations and by proposing a
prioritized set of directions for future improvement.
