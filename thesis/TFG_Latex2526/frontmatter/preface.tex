\frontmattertitle{Preface}

\noindent
This document presents the bachelor's thesis completed during the 2025--2026 academic year within the Bachelor's Degree in Mathematical Engineering in Data Science at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, in collaboration with the Dimension Lab at Hospital de la Santa Creu i Sant Pau.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
The main body of the thesis (Introduction through Conclusions) is written in English for consistency with the scientific literature in the field.

\frontmattertitle{Use of Generative AI Disclosure}

\noindent
The author, Adri\`{a} Cort\'{e}s Cugat, discloses that the preparation of this thesis, ``Automated 3D Stenosis Quantification and Visualization Workflow for Coronary Artery Disease Clinical Decision Support,'' involved the use of generative AI tools. \textbf{Cursor AI} and \textbf{Google Gemini} were used in the writing of this report for language review, text editing, technical writing support, and \LaTeX{} integration and formatting. The same tools were also used during the project for the purpose of: contextual clinical understanding and research process; software development assistance; code debugging and optimization.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
The author warrants that the contents of this thesis are a genuine human creation: all outputs obtained from these tools have been carefully reviewed and edited by Adri\`{a} Cort\'{e}s Cugat. In accordance with university guidelines on the use of AI in assessable work, the technical and clinical substance of the main body, especially the Introduction, Methods, and Conclusions, has been written, validated, and revised by the author with the support of the thesis director and supervisor.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent
\textbf{Information source disclosure.} The author likewise declares that all cited sources have been read and reviewed by the author and are sufficiently contrasted so as to be deemed reliable.
